# Two-Sentence Horror

A two-sentence horror story for the make.fun bounty "Two-Sentence Horror".
`main.py` writes the story to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`).

The story: a boy born without a voice knocks on his bedroom wall —
twice for yes, once for no — and the house has slept for nine years to
his small, patient questions. He has been at his grandmother's since
Tuesday, but the knocking has not stopped, and last night it learned a
third knock, and a fourth, and began, very slowly, to spell.
